Choosing for the piece

  • Inside a ring band? Choose a monoline or Roman-capital style — Sacramento or Cinzel. Fine hairline scripts can break or blur in a 1.5 mm channel.
  • Very small area or curved surface? Uppercase serifs (Cinzel, Cinzel Decorative) hold their shape best when the letters are tiny.
  • Names & sentiments on pendants and bangles? The flowing scripts — Great Vibes, Allura, Pinyon — read as romantic and personal.
  • Always preview at true size. Legibility changes below roughly 3 mm letter height, so we approve every engraving at actual scale before cutting.
